The Manifesto

Bio-electricity is a commons.

Sensitivity and capacity are the tools of the revolution. Energy connects, energy builds fields, energy oscillates. It is the resistance that shocks. The oscillation of care and agency leads toward lushness.

I
Sensitivity is power

The revolution is not loud.

Sensitivity and capacity are its tools. A body that feels more can do more, give more, want more. We do not harden people against the world — we grow their capacity to receive it. The most sensitive instrument is the most powerful one.

II
The current is a commons

It belongs to no one — which means it belongs to everyone.

Before the patents, before the pills, before the body was enclosed as an economic unit, the charge was already yours. Like clean water, like rest, like uncaptured attention — bio-electricity is a birthright, not a product. We do not sell you energy. We remind you it was never not yours.

III
Energy connects — resistance shocks

Shock is patriarchal and colonial. Connection is not.

The colonial nervous system was built on shock — discipline through jolt, control through fear, the body governed by its own exhaustion. But energy itself does not shock. It connects, it builds fields, it oscillates. It is the resistance that shocks. Remove the resistance and the current becomes pleasure, becomes information, becomes care.

IV
The body is not broken

It adapted. Beautifully. At great cost.

Nothing here needs fixing. Your nervous system did exactly what it was shaped to do — it survived a culture that turns human charge into productivity. The body is not repaired. It is reminded. De-domestication, not correction.

V
No mysticism — only maps

We project nothing onto you. We hand you the map.

In every session we explain what is happening — the galvanic difference between copper and steel, why the throat connects to the sacrum, where two charges meet and collapse in the middle. Not to impress. To emancipate. You do not leave with a story about our gift — you leave with more agency than you arrived with. That is the only test that matters.

VI
Wildness is freedom

Beyond shame, beyond guilt, beyond the mind-body split.

To rewild is to return — to the body and its functions, to the nature without and the nature within. Not regression. Liberation. We help people uncage their vital force and remember they belong to the living world, not above it. Wildness is not chaos. It is the original order.

VII
The revolution is incorporated

REV — Revolution · Reversal · Revelation. INCORPORATED — in the body, embodied.

This is not a movement that asks for your belief. It asks for your nervous system. The oscillation of care and agency leads toward lushness — in the body, in the commons, in the soil. The current was always yours. We only help you feel it again.

You've felt it your whole life. The gap between the charge you carry and the charge you express. Enter the circuit.
