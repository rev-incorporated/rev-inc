01 — The Terrain

A battery only charges in the right medium.

Before current, terrain. An acidic, dehydrated body resists its own charge. Lime juice and apple cider vinegar shift the stomach toward alkaline. Himalayan salt and coconut water rehydrate at the cellular level. Collagen peptides feed myelin — the insulation that lets a nerve signal travel clean instead of leaking. Alkalise, hydrate, insulate. Then the body receives current instead of fighting it.

02 — The Minerals

Electrolytes are the wires.

Every nerve impulse is mineral ions moving across a membrane. Potassium, magnesium, sodium, calcium — these are not supplements, they are the conductive medium. Low minerals, low conductivity: the signal weakens not because you lack willpower but because the wire is thin. Restore the four salts and the body's own electricity flows again. Creatine then fuels the ATP cycle that turns charge into work.

03 — The Metals

Copper, steel, and the galvanic gap.

Copper and steel sit at different points on the galvanic series. Hold one in each hand and the difference between them creates a measurable potential — a tiny battery, with you as the circuit. This is a different mechanism from the microvolt of bio-electricity: galvanic potential plus magnetism, not nerve current. The pharaonic rods let you hold both metals and become your own inner interface while in circuit — feeling the charge move rather than being told it does.
